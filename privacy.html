Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Jabble does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone, except the scores you choose to post to Apple's Game Center.

This policy covers the Jabble app for iPhone ("Jabble", "the app"), made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. We do not run servers for Jabble, and the app does not send any information to us or to third parties for analytics, advertising or any other purpose.

Information stored on your device

Jabble saves a few things on your iPhone so the app works the way you left it:

  • your game settings (grid size, timer and shortest word),
  • the name you choose to show to other players,
  • your best solo score for each grid size,
  • which app icon you've picked.

This stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Camera

When you join a friend's game, Jabble can use the camera to scan their QR code. The camera image is processed on your iPhone only to read the code; nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can type the code instead, and you can turn camera access off at any time in Settings.

Nearby multiplayer (Bluetooth and local network)

Multiplayer games connect phones directly to each other over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity. During a game, the phones exchange only what the game needs: the display name each player chose, the letter board and settings, the words each player found and the scores. This goes directly between the phones in the game, not through the internet or any server, and none of it is kept after the game ends.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Jabble uses it to show your Game Center name and to post your solo scores to the Jabble leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). We can see leaderboard scores and Game Center nicknames, as any player can, but we don't receive your Apple ID or contact details. You can sign out of Game Center in the iPhone Settings app; Jabble works fully without it.

Children

Jabble is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about your data in Game Center, Apple's privacy page explains how to manage it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts to handle data differently, we'll update this page and change the date at the top before the new version is released.
