How to play

  • Drag across touching letters (diagonals count) and lift your finger to submit a word.
  • Each letter can be used once per word. Slide back over the last letter to undo it.
  • "Qu" is a single tile and counts as two letters.
  • Points: 3–4 letters = 1, 5 = 2, 6 = 3, 7 = 5, 8 or more = 11.
  • Words that use the green tile earn a bonus point.
  • Find 5 words within 5 seconds for double points for 10 seconds — the timer flashes while it's on. Do it again for triple.

Common questions

Why didn't my word count?

Jabble uses a curated list of common English words (US spelling). Proper nouns, abbreviations and swear words aren't included, and words shorter than your "shortest word" setting don't count. If you think a word is missing, email us and we'll look at adding it.

How do I play with friends?

One person taps Host game and shows their QR code. Everyone else taps Join game and scans it (or types the code). Phones need to be near each other with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi switched on — you don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi network, and no internet is needed.

My friend can't find my game.

Check that both phones have Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on and that Jabble is allowed to use the local network (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Jabble). Keep the phones close together and the host's screen on until everyone has joined.

The camera won't scan the code.

Make sure Jabble has camera access in Settings → Jabble → Camera. You can always type the 6-character code instead.

Why don't some of my words score in multiplayer?

Words that more than one player found score zero, so it pays to look for the less obvious ones.

How do leaderboards work?

When you're signed in to Game Center, each finished solo round posts your score to the leaderboard for that grid size. Tap the trophy on the home screen to see them. To sign in, open the Settings app → Game Center.

How do I change the app icon?

Tap the gear on the home screen, then turn on Wedding edition under App icon. Turn it off to go back to the original.

Does Jabble collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. See the privacy policy for details.
